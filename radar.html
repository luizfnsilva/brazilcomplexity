What it is

A reading of the regime, not a set of tips

Radar Perene is a regulatory and market intelligence instrument for understanding Brazil's operational environment. Where most tools offer signals, Radar Perene reads the environment descriptively, from public data — monitoring Brazil's risk regime and the global one, regulatory and judicial activity across electoral, tax, real estate, succession and criminal domains, and the intermarket picture.

It shows what is happening, with depth that scales from the basic to the advanced. It is the same operational layer that informs the research published here — now instrumented.

Common questions

About Radar Perene

What is Radar Perene?

A regulatory and market intelligence instrument for understanding Brazil's operational environment. It reads the regime descriptively, from public data — monitoring Brazil's risk regime, regulatory and judicial activity, and intermarket conditions. Educational content, not investment advice.

Is Radar Perene investment advice?

No. Radar Perene is a descriptive reading of regime and context from public sources. It does not recommend purchases, predict returns, or provide investment, legal, or tax advice. Its purpose is to surface what is happening in the environment, with the contradictions left visible.

When does Radar Perene launch?

Radar Perene is in pre-launch. The live preview on this page shows what the instrument computes. Early access is available by writing to hello@brazilcomplexity.com, and the full platform will be available at radarperene.com.

How does Radar Perene relate to the Brazil Complexity Brief?

The Radar reads the environment at the macro and intermarket level. The Brief applies analysis to a specific decision — a municipality, an asset, a sector. The Radar is context; the Brief is the deal.
